E-COMMERCE INVENTORY RULES
==========================

Rule 1:
If product stock is greater than zero,
then the product is available.

Rule 2:
If product stock is equal to zero,
then the product is out of stock.

Rule 3:
If the product is available and payment is successful,
then the order can be confirmed.

Rule 4:
If the product is out of stock,
then the order should be cancelled or backordered.
